Uncompromising artisanal practices passed down through three generations of Grasse master perfumers.

01

DAWN HARVEST

Centifolia roses and wild orchids hand-gathered at 5:30 AM while morning dew still protects the fragile floral nectar oils.

02

ALEMBIC EXTRACTION

Slow fractional steam distillation conducted in custom hammered-copper alembic stills at low temperature to preserve pure natural terpenes.

180 days of slow cellar maturation where raw fossilized amber resin marries with smoky agarwood into an unctuous golden concentrate.
